Services

Three Ways
to Work Together

All support happens online, so we can work with facilitators wherever they are. Every engagement begins with a conversation about your retreat and what you need.

01

Inclusion Review

A thoughtful first step

A careful look at how your retreat welcomes people, before anyone arrives. You receive a short written report with clear, practical recommendations.

  • Retreat page and promotional materials
  • Registration and intake forms
  • Policies and participant agreements
  • Accessibility, consent, privacy, and pricing access
02

Retreat Support

From launch to follow-up

Remote administrative support for one retreat, from the day registration opens to the last thank you note.

  • Registration and intake form setup
  • Participant emails and reminders
  • Payment tracking
  • A pre-retreat welcome packet
  • A post-retreat survey
Optional marketing add-on

Retreat page copy, a short launch email series, and social posts that sound like you.

03

Monthly Support

Steady help between retreats

A set number of hours each month for facilitators who want ongoing support without hiring a full-time assistant.

  • Client and participant communication
  • Scheduling and follow-up
  • Newsletters and social posts
  • Simple systems that keep things moving

Seven Feathers focuses on remote support. We do not coordinate venues, vendors, travel, or on-site staffing, which keeps our attention where it serves you best.

Our Values

The Seven Feathers

Feathers are sacred across many traditions. They are given, not taken, and honored, not possessed. Our seven feathers shape how we partner with facilitators, how we care for every participant, and how we help retreats grow in ways that are ethical and sustainable. Select a feather to read more.

We come alongside your work, never over it. Whether you guide yoga, grief circles, breathwork, creative writing, or leadership intensives, we honor your methods and the way you hold space.

In practice: we learn your voice before we write a word for you, and we never reshape your offering into something it is not.

Good support is a relationship, not a transaction. We aim for agreements where everyone gives and receives in balance: you, your participants, venues, vendors, and the communities that host you.

In practice: clear, fair pricing, honest scopes of work, and a preference for local businesses and practitioners wherever we can.

Facilitators often lose the joy of their work under emails, spreadsheets, and logistics. When we carry the details, you get your energy back for the part only you can do.

In practice: we take on the tasks that drain you and build simple systems so they stop piling up. We also help you plan retreats with real rest built in, for participants and for you.

Every retreat lives within a web of relationships: facilitators, participants, venues, cooks, cleaners, local practitioners, and the land itself. We tend those connections with care.

In practice: warm, timely communication with everyone involved, and thoughtful introductions when we know someone who could serve your work.

Inclusion belongs in the design of a retreat from the very beginning. We help you shape gatherings where people of different bodies, backgrounds, identities, abilities, and budgets can arrive fully.

In practice: guidance on accessible venues and materials, welcoming language, dietary and sensory needs, pronouns, and pricing options such as sliding scale or scholarship spots.

Years spent walking alongside survivors of violence taught us that healing only takes root when a person's boundaries and choices are honored. We bring that understanding to every retreat we support.

In practice: consent centered, trauma informed practices, clear participant agreements, careful handling of personal information, and confidentiality you can count on. You also keep full ownership of your business, your content, and your client relationships.

Dignity means no one has to struggle or shrink to take part. Accessibility is how we honor it, for the people who attend a retreat and for the people who lead it.

Facilitators deserve accommodations too. We shape our support around the way you naturally think and work, never a cookie cutter template. Every mind works a little differently, and we welcome that. We adapt to your pace, your energy, and the way you take in information best.

In practice: tools and materials tailored to how each client works best, including visual, audio, and large print formats, along with clear, predictable communication and breaks built into every schedule.

The Founder

Amy Rohan

Founder, Seven Feathers

For more than thirty years, Amy Rohan has helped people and organizations run more simply. She has a keen eye for detail and a gift for anticipating needs, often solving a problem before it ever reaches the room.

Years of walking alongside survivors of violence taught her how to create spaces where people feel safe and free to be themselves. She brings that same care to every facilitator she supports, grounded in a degree in psychology, training in trauma and inclusion, and her practice as a certified Reiki Master. Her Indigenous roots shape a deep respect for the land and a commitment to work that serves generations to come.
